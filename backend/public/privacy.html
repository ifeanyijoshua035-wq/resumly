1. Information we collect

Account information. When you register, we collect your name, email address, and password. Your password is never stored in plain text — it's hashed using bcrypt before being saved, so we ourselves cannot read it.

Profile information. You may optionally add a phone number, location, and (on paid plans) a personal website or portfolio link.

Content you create. Resumes, cover letters, and job application entries you build in the Service — which may include your full employment history, education, contact details, and other personal information you choose to include. This content is yours; we store it so you can access and edit it later.

Payment information. Payments are processed by Flutterwave. We do not receive or store your card number or other full payment credentials — Flutterwave shares with us only what's needed to manage your subscription (transaction status, amount, currency, and a payment reference). Flutterwave's own privacy policy governs how it handles your payment details directly.

Location / IP address. When you start a Premium checkout, we use your IP address to determine your country, via the IP-geolocation service ipinfo.io, solely to apply the correct regional price. We do not otherwise track or store a history of your location.

AI features. If you use the AI Assistant, cover letter generator, or ATS score checker, the relevant text (such as a job description you paste, or resume content you ask the AI to work with) is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. This is subject to Google's own API data-handling terms in addition to this policy.

Usage and device data. Standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and access times, collected automatically as part of running the Service and keeping it secure (for example, to apply rate limits that prevent abuse).

Local storage. We use your browser's local storage (not third-party tracking cookies) to keep you logged in, and remember your display preferences such as dark mode and language.
